## Brand & Style

The brand evokes an atmosphere of quiet luxury, curatorial precision, and bespoke craftsmanship. Targeted at discerning collectors and connoisseurs of high-end, slow-fashion tailoring, the interface communicates restraint, permanence, and editorial authority.

The aesthetic fuses **Modern Editorial Minimalism** with tactile, architectural structure. High visual contrast between stark typographic statements and warm, neutral surface layers evokes high-grade silk, raw cashmere, and architectural concrete. The design prioritizes generous negative space, deliberate proportions, sharp alignment, and subtle transitions over ornamentation. Imagery takes center stage, framed by razor-thin borders, tactile micro-interactions, and pristine typographic hierarchy.

## Colors

The palette is rooted in timeless tactile materials, prioritizing high legibility, accessible contrast ratios, and warm, textural neutrals over cold digital grays.

- **Primary (`#0F0F10`) — Obsidian Black**: Deep, saturated mineral black used for display headlines, primary active states, structural borders, and stark high-impact CTA surfaces.
- **Secondary (`#C5A880`) — Champagne Gold**: A subdued, brushed metallic tone reserved strictly for curated focal points: active indicators, exclusive editorial badges, refined iconography, and subtle hover borders.
- **Tertiary (`#E8E4DC`) — Warm Sand**: An organic mid-tone neutral used for structural hairline dividers, subtle container backgrounds, image frame borders, and interactive card hovers.
- **Neutral (`#F5F3EF`) — Warm Greige / Raw Cashmere**: The primary canvas background color. Replaces blinding stark digital whites with an organic, textile-inspired undertone.
- **Surface Pure (`#FFFFFF`) — Crisp Silk White**: Deployed selectively on floating overlays, cart panels, navigation backdrops, and active form controls to create clean architectural lift.
- **Text Muted (`#6E6B65`) — Raw Slate**: Meets WCAG AAA compliance against greige canvas, utilized for metadata, captions, secondary specs, and SKUs.

## Typography

The typographical pairing unites high-fashion editorial gravitas with sharp, functional utility.

- **Headlines & Editorial Statements (`Bodoni Moda`)**: Utilizes extreme stroke contrast and classic serifs to command visual attention in campaign lookbooks, product collection names, and hero showcases. Display sizes should always employ tight tracking to emulate haute couture print publications.
- **Product UI, Body & Pricing (`Hanken Grotesk`)**: Provides uncompromised clarity, geometric precision, and legibility across dense data tables, product specifications, bag overlays, and sizing guides.
- **Uppercase Hierarchy**: Labels, collection tags, micro-badges, and CTA buttons adopt `label-md` or `label-sm` set in full uppercase with expanded letter-spacing (`0.08em` to `0.16em`) to enforce an architectural cadence throughout the shopping journey.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout model is governed by an asymmetric, highly disciplined fluid grid structured around luxury editorial print standards:

- **Grid Architecture**: 12-column grid on desktop (max-width `1680px`), transitioning to an 8-column layout on tablet (`768px` – `1024px`), and a 4-column layout on mobile (`<768px`).
- **Pacing & Rhythms**: Negative space functions as a structural element rather than void. Full-width lookbook panels alternate with offset 5-column and 7-column content pairings, breaking standard symmetrical e-commerce monotony.
- **Viewport Fluidity**: Outer margins scale progressively from `1rem` on mobile devices up to `6rem` on ultra-wide viewports, ensuring edge-to-edge luxury framing without edge crowding.
- **Section Breaks**: Collections and narrative chapters must utilize large macro-spacing (`space-2xl` to `space-3xl`) to establish deliberate pacing that encourages leisurely browsing.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy eschews heavy physical drop shadows in favor of architectural surface layering, translucent frosted glassmorphism, and hairline micro-borders.

- **Micro-Border Framing**: Depth is delineated using 1px hairline borders (`#E8E4DC` on light greige canvas, `#0F0F10` at 15% opacity on white surfaces). These provide crisp structural segregation without visual bulk.
- **Sticky Architectural Navigation**: The primary header utilizes a semi-translucent backdrop (`rgba(245, 243, 239, 0.85)`) coupled with a heavy `backdrop-filter: blur(20px)` and a bottom border of `rgba(15, 15, 16, 0.08)`.
- **Flyouts & Modals**: Slide-over cart drawers and quick-view panels adopt `#FFFFFF` with an ambient, ultra-diffused drop shadow: `0px 24px 48px -12px rgba(15, 15, 16, 0.08)`.
- **Image Elevations**: Product cards and campaign tiles maintain zero elevation resting states. Hover interactions evoke subtle 1.015x image scale transitions within a fixed-boundary clipping container, accompanied by a subtle `#C5A880` inner stroke highlight.

## Shapes

The foundational shape language is purely architectural and sharp (`roundedness: 0`), reflecting high-fashion tailoring, editorial magazines, and marble plinths. 

- **Containers & Structural Elements**: Product cards, banners, accordions, inputs, tables, and dropdown menus utilize razor-sharp `0px` radii, emphasizing structural precision.
- **Exceptions (Pill Badges & Size Selectors)**: To juxtapose against the linear geometry, micro-status tags (e.g., "ARCHIVE", "PRE-ORDER", "1 OF 50") and product sizing toggles adopt a 9999px fully rounded pill shape. This intentional tension between sharp rectangular framing and soft pill accents mirrors atelier garment tags and luxury jewelry silhouettes.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary**: Solid obsidian black (`#0F0F10`) background, crisp silk white (`#FFFFFF`) text, sharp 0px corners, uppercase `label-md` typography. Padding: `1rem 2rem`. Hover: transforms background to `#C5A880` with seamless 300ms cubic-bezier transitions.
- **Secondary / Ghost**: Transparent background, 1px solid `#0F0F10` hairline border, `#0F0F10` text. Hover: fills with `#0F0F10` background and `#FFFFFF` text.
- **Editorial Link**: Borderless; styled with `label-md`, accompanied by an elongated hairline arrow (`→`) that translates 4px horizontally on hover with an expanding underline indicator.

### Product Cards
- Aspect ratio locked to `3:4` or `4:5` vertical portrait dimensions.
- Zero border radius with 1px hairline border in `#E8E4DC`.
- Clean visual layout: primary imagery displays editorial still life; hover seamlessly fades to secondary on-model movement shot.
- Floating upper-left tag container housing pill badges. Floating bottom-right quick-add floating trigger on mouse hover.
- Typographic stack beneath image: Bodoni Moda collection/garment name (`headline-sm`), followed by muted category metadata (`body-sm`), terminating in tabular numerals for pricing (`label-lg`).

### Form Controls & Inputs
- **Text Inputs**: Minimalist bottom-border-only style or ultra-clean 1px boxed field in `#E8E4DC` over `#FFFFFF`. Focus state deepens border to `#0F0F10` with zero outline glow. Floating labels in `label-sm`.
- **Size & Swatch Selectors**: Material swatches use 24px circular discs with a 1px offset ring when selected. Garment sizes (XS, S, M, L, XL) reside within pill-shaped capsules (`rounded-full`) that reverse from white/greige to obsidian black upon selection.
- **Checkboxes & Radios**: Minimalist 16px square/circle with crisp 1px borders. Checked state features a solid obsidian `#0F0F10` interior fill with an optical white geometric glyph.

### Badges & Pill Tags
- Scaled down (`label-sm`), height `22px`, padding `0.2rem 0.75rem`.
- Background options: Translucent Greige (`rgba(245, 243, 239, 0.9)`) with `#0F0F10` text, or subtle Champagne Gold tint (`rgba(197, 168, 128, 0.15)`) with `#8A6D46` text for limited editions.

### Cart Drawer & Modals
- Slides smoothly from the right viewport boundary over a dimmed ambient overlay (`rgba(15, 15, 16, 0.4)` backdrop blur `4px`).
- Header features crisp editorial typography with item count and hairline separator.
- Line items showcase micro 1:1 image thumbnails, quantity counter with hairline dividers, and instantaneous subtotal recalculations.
